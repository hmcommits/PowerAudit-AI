BESCOM (BANGALORE ELECTRICITY SUPPLY COMPANY)
TARIFF ORDER 2024 — KERC (Karnataka Electricity Regulatory Commission)
Clause 11(a) — Maximum Demand Charges and Penal Provisions

Applicability: All High Tension (HT) consumers under HT-2(a), HT-2(b), HT-2(c), HT-3, and EHT categories.

Contract Demand Compliance:
  Consumers must operate within their sanctioned Contract Demand (CD) at all times.
  If the Maximum Demand (MD) recorded in any 15-minute interval in a billing month exceeds the sanctioned Contract Demand:

Penalty Formula:
  If Recorded MD > Contract Demand x 1.10:
    Excess Demand (kVA) = Recorded MD - Contract Demand
    Penal Charge = Excess Demand (kVA) x Applicable Demand Rate x 2.0

  The applicable demand rate is Rs. 230/kVA/month for HT-2(a) (industrial) category consumers.

  BESCOM strictly enforces the 2x (double) penal rate — the highest multiplier among the three DISCOMs.
  Source: Whitepaper confirms: "If Maximum Demand exceeds sanctioned load, penal charges apply at 2x the normal rate."

  Practical Implication for Large Factories:
  A factory with CD = 600 kVA and MD spike to 700 kVA in one 15-minute window:
    Excess = 100 kVA; Penalty = 100 x 230 x 2 = Rs. 46,000 for that billing month.
  Install IoT-based energy management systems to sequence heavy motor starts and prevent MD spikes.

Minimum Demand Provision:
  If MD < 50% of CD in any month, the consumer is billed for 50% of CD as Minimum Demand.
  Minimum Demand Charge = CD x 0.50 x Rs. 230 per kVA

Off-Season Tariff:
  BESCOM allows industrial consumers to avail of an off-season tariff for up to six (6) months per year during overhauling/maintenance periods. This significantly reduces minimum billing requirements during low-production periods.

Reference: BESCOM Tariff Order 2024, KERC Tariff Order, Clause 11(a) — Maximum Demand Charges.

BESCOM TARIFF ORDER 2024
Clause 12 — Power Factor Incentive and Penalty

All HT consumers must maintain an average Power Factor of 0.90 or above throughout the billing month.

Power Factor Penalty:
  If average PF < 0.90:
  Penalty = Energy Charges x ((0.90 - Recorded PF) / 0.90) x 1.00

Power Factor Incentive:
  If average PF >= 0.95:
  Incentive = Energy Charges x ((Recorded PF - 0.95) / 0.95) x 0.50
  The incentive amount is credited to the consumer's next bill.

Recommendation: Install Automatic Power Factor Correction (APFC) panels to maintain PF >= 0.95 consistently. This avoids penalties and generates DISCOM credits, effectively reducing the energy bill.

Reference: BESCOM Tariff Order 2024, Clause 12 — Power Factor Incentive/Penalty.

BESCOM TARIFF ORDER 2024
Time-of-Day (ToD) Mandatory Provisions

ToD tariff is MANDATORY for the following consumer categories:
  - HT-2(a): Industrial consumers with Contract Demand >= 500 kVA
  - HT-2(b): Industrial consumers with Contract Demand >= 500 kVA
  - HT-2(c): Industrial consumers with Contract Demand >= 500 kVA

  ToD tariff remains OPTIONAL for consumers with Contract Demand below 500 kVA.

Off-Peak Hours: Typically 10 PM to 6 AM (22:00 to 06:00)
Peak Hours: 6 AM to 10 PM

Strategic Recommendation: Shift energy-intensive operations (crushing, heavy heating, batch processing) to off-peak hours (10 PM - 6 AM) to leverage ToD discounts. This is most impactful for manufacturers.

Reference: BESCOM Tariff Order 2024, ToD Schedule.

BESCOM TARIFF ORDER 2024
LT-6(a) Category — Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Tariff

EV Charging Tariff: Rs. 5.50 to Rs. 6.50 per unit (single-part tariff, LT-6a category)
  Exact rate within this band depends on connection type and CPO agreement.

  NO DEMAND CHARGES: EV charging connections under LT-6(a) are completely exempt from fixed demand charges. This is consistent with the national policy of promoting EV infrastructure by reducing fixed OPEX for Charge Point Operators (CPOs).

  BESCOM EV Land Aggregator Portal: BESCOM operates a dedicated portal under the PM E-DRIVE initiative to connect private landowners with CPOs seeking to deploy public charging infrastructure.

  Best Target Segment: Tech parks, public highway corridors, and urban parking facilities in Bengaluru.

  Critical Compliance Note: Never charge EVs on a standard HT-2 commercial or industrial meter. EV loads can cause sudden Maximum Demand spikes, triggering the 2x MD penal rate (Clause 11(a)). Always apply for a dedicated LT-6(a) EV meter.

Reference: BESCOM Tariff Order 2024, LT-6(a) — EV Charging Category.
